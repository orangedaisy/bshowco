### [Baseball Scorecard](/blog/keeping-score/)

This year I designed my own scorecard so I could keep score of Cubs' games on
my iPad. It works pretty well in print, too. I took inspiration from many
scorecard product options, a pair of lovely fonts from Dan Cederholm, and
iterated through multiple designs until I landed on something that perfectly
suits what I was looking for.
